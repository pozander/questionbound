# Query 04: Marxist Philosophy of Mind + AI

## Purpose

Looks for work in Marxist philosophy of mind, Marxist theories of consciousness, and materialist theories of consciousness applied to AI, machine consciousness, or LLMs.

## Search Query

```text
("Marxist philosophy of mind" OR "Marxist theory of mind" OR "Marxist theory of consciousness" OR "materialist theory of consciousness")
AND
("artificial intelligence" OR AI OR "machine intelligence" OR "machine consciousness" OR "artificial consciousness" OR "large language model*" OR LLM*)
AND
(thinking OR cognition OR intentionality OR understanding OR subjectivity OR agency)
NOT
(Vygotsky OR Leontiev OR Leontyev OR Ilyenkov OR Il'enkov OR "activity theory" OR "cultural-historical psychology")
```
