# Query 08: Broader Discovery Query

## Purpose

Deliberately loose query for Google Scholar, Semantic Scholar, or early exploratory searching where strict Boolean syntax may hide relevant literature.

## Search Query

```text
("artificial intelligence" OR "machine intelligence" OR "thinking machines" OR "large language models")
AND
(Marxism OR Marxist OR Leninist OR "dialectical materialism" OR "Soviet philosophy")
AND
(consciousness OR cognition OR thought OR mind)
```
