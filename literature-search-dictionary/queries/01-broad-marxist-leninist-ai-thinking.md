# Query 01: Broad Marxist-Leninist AI Thinking

## Purpose

Broad first-pass search for literature connecting contemporary artificial intelligence with Marxism-Leninism, dialectical materialism, Soviet philosophy, thought, cognition, and consciousness.

## Search Query

```text
("artificial intelligence" OR AI OR "large language model*" OR LLM* OR "generative AI" OR "foundation model*" OR "machine intelligence" OR "machine cognition" OR "machine thinking")
AND
("Marxism-Leninism" OR "Marxist-Leninist" OR "dialectical materialism" OR "materialist dialectics" OR "Soviet philosophy" OR "Soviet Marxism")
AND
(thought OR thinking OR cognition OR consciousness OR mind OR understanding)
NOT
(Vygotsky OR Leontiev OR Leontyev OR Ilyenkov OR Il'enkov OR "activity theory" OR "cultural-historical psychology")
```
