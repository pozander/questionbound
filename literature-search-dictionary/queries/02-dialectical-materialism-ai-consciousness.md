# Query 02: Dialectical Materialism + AI Consciousness

## Purpose

Targets literature that explicitly uses dialectical materialism, materialist dialectics, or dialectical logic to discuss AI, cognition, consciousness, intentionality, or understanding.

## Search Query

```text
("dialectical materialism" OR "materialist dialectics" OR "dialectical logic")
AND
("artificial intelligence" OR "machine intelligence" OR "large language model*" OR LLM* OR "deep learning" OR "neural network*" OR "foundation model*")
AND
(consciousness OR cognition OR thought OR thinking OR understanding OR intentionality)
NOT
(Vygotsky OR Leontiev OR Leontyev OR Ilyenkov OR Il'enkov OR "activity theory" OR "cultural-historical psychology")
```
