# Query 06: Machines, Thought, and Materialism

## Purpose

Catches broader philosophy-of-machine-thinking debates that use materialism, Marxism, Leninism, or Soviet philosophy without necessarily naming contemporary AI first.

## Search Query

```text
("machine thinking" OR "thinking machines" OR "can machines think" OR "machine consciousness" OR "artificial consciousness")
AND
(materialism OR "dialectical materialism" OR Marxist OR Leninist OR "Soviet philosophy")
AND
(thought OR consciousness OR cognition OR mind OR understanding)
NOT
(Vygotsky OR Leontiev OR Leontyev OR Ilyenkov OR Il'enkov OR "activity theory" OR "cultural-historical psychology")
```
