# Query 05: Historical Materialism / Social Being + AI Thought

## Purpose

Useful for testing whether thought or consciousness is theorized as requiring social labor, social relations, praxis, or practical activity.

## Search Query

```text
("historical materialism" OR "social being" OR praxis OR "social practice" OR labor OR labour)
AND
(consciousness OR cognition OR thought OR thinking OR subjectivity)
AND
("artificial intelligence" OR AI OR "large language model*" OR LLM* OR "machine intelligence" OR "machine learning")
NOT
(Vygotsky OR Leontiev OR Leontyev OR Ilyenkov OR Il'enkov OR "activity theory" OR "cultural-historical psychology")
```
