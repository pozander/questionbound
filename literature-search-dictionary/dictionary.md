# Literature Search Dictionary

## Topic

Whether contemporary artificial intelligence systems, especially large language models and related machine-learning systems, can be said to think from a Marxist-Leninist or dialectical materialist theoretical basis.

## Deliberate Exclusions

This dictionary is intended to support searches for alternatives to the Vygotsky-Leontiev-Ilyenkov/activity-theory cluster.

Use these exclusion terms where supported:

```text
NOT Vygotsky
NOT Leontiev
NOT Leontyev
NOT Ilyenkov
NOT Il'enkov
NOT "activity theory"
NOT "cultural-historical psychology"
```
